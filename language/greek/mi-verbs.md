# 🧠 DEEP DIVE: Attic Greek Μι-Verbs (Root-Verbs)

---

## 🧩 1. What are μι-verbs?

**μι-verbs** (also called **root verbs**) are a class of verbs in Attic Greek that **do not follow the thematic (ω-verbs)** conjugation pattern. Instead of adding a connecting vowel (ο/ε), they often **attach personal endings directly to the root**.

They are called “μι-verbs” because their **present active indicative first-person singular** ends in **-μι** (e.g., δίδωμι, τίθημι, ἵστημι, ἵημι).

---

## 🧱 2. Key Characteristics of μι-verbs

| Feature                        | μι-verbs                                           | Thematic (ω) Verbs                   |
|-------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| 1st sg. Pres. Act. Ind.       | -μι (e.g. δίδωμι)                                 | -ω (e.g. παιδεύω)                    |
| Connecting Vowel              | None in some forms                                | ο/ε                                  |
| Present Stem                  | Often **reduplicated**                            | Usually not reduplicated             |
| Imperfect                     | Uses augment + imperfect endings (e.g., ἐδίδουν)  | Same, but with thematic vowel        |
| Tense Stems                   | Often **suppletive** or irregular across tenses   | Usually consistent                    |
| Personal Endings              | Use **athematic** endings in many forms           | Use thematic endings                 |

---

## 🧪 3. Principal Parts of μι-verbs

Like all Greek verbs, μι-verbs have **six principal parts**. However, for many μι-verbs, the stems across these parts are irregular or suppletive.

Example: **δίδωμι** ("I give")

| Part | Principal Part           | Tense                | Notes                             |
|------|--------------------------|----------------------|-----------------------------------|
| 1    | δίδωμι                   | Present              | Reduplication of stem: **δι-**    |
| 2    | δώσω                     | Future               | Different stem                    |
| 3    | ἔδωκα                    | Aorist Active        | Suppletive form                   |
| 4    | δέδωκα                   | Perfect Active       | Reduplication again               |
| 5    | δέδομαι                  | Perfect Middle/Pass. | Middle formation from perf stem   |
| 6    | ἐδόθην                   | Aorist Passive       | Passive stem                      |

---

## 🧬 4. Common μι-verbs to Know

These are the most frequent and irregular μι-verbs in Attic Greek:

| Verb      | Meaning       | Notes                              |
|-----------|---------------|------------------------------------|
| δίδωμι    | I give        | Extremely irregular; high frequency |
| τίθημι    | I place       | Similar structure to δίδωμι         |
| ἵημι     | I send/let go | Very irregular; many contractions  |
| ἵστημι    | I set/stand   | Semantically complex, has intransitive forms |
| δείκνυμι  | I show        | μι-verb with present in -νυμι       |
| ἀφίημι    | I release     | Compound of ἵημι                   |
| ἐπιτίθημι | I place upon  | Compound of τίθημι                 |

## 🏗 5. Morphology of μι-verbs

Let’s now examine each major form and formation:

### 🔹 Present Indicative Active (δίδωμι)

| Person     | Form      |
|------------|-----------|
| 1st sg     | δίδωμι    |
| 2nd sg     | δίδως     |
| 3rd sg     | δίδωσι(ν) |
| 1st pl     | δίδομεν   |
| 2nd pl     | δίδοτε    |
| 3rd pl     | διδόασι(ν)|

Notes:
- Reduplication: δι- + stem δ + personal endings.
- No thematic vowel before endings.

### 🔹 Imperfect Active (ἐδίδουν)

| Person     | Form       |
|------------|------------|
| 1st sg     | ἐδίδουν     |
| 2nd sg     | ἐδίδους     |
| 3rd sg     | ἐδίδου      |
| 1st pl     | ἐδίδομεν    |
| 2nd pl     | ἐδίδοτε     |
| 3rd pl     | ἐδίδοσαν    |

Note:
- Augment + reduplicated present stem + secondary endings.

### 🔹 Aorist Active (ἔδωκα)

| Person     | Form     |
|------------|----------|
| 1st sg     | ἔδωκα    |
| 2nd sg     | ἔδωκας   |
| 3rd sg     | ἔδωκε(ν) |
| 1st pl     | ἔδομεν   |
| 2nd pl     | ἔδοτε    |
| 3rd pl     | ἔδοσαν   |

Very irregular; not based on the present stem!

## 🧠 6. Syntax and Usage of μι-verbs

μi-verbs, because of their high frequency, show up in nearly all syntactic environments:

### ✅ Direct Object (Accusative)
> **δίδωμί** σοι **τὸ βιβλίον**  
> *I give you the book.*  
> → accusative direct object

### ✅ Dative of Indirect Object
> **δίδωμί** τῷ παιδὶ **δῶρον**  
> *I give the child a gift.*  
> → triple construction: subject, indirect obj, direct obj

### ✅ Infinitives
> **βούλομαι δοῦναι** τὸ δῶρον.  
> *I want to give the gift.*  
> → δοῦναι = aorist infinitive of δίδωμι

### ✅ Subjunctive
> **ἵνα δῷ** τοῖς φίλοις  
> *so that he may give to his friends*  
> → δῷ: aorist subjunctive, used after ἵνα

---

## 🔁 7. μι-Verbs vs Thematic Verbs

Some verbs have both μι- and ω- variants (especially in compounds or poetic registers). Others transition to thematic forms over time (Koine Greek shows this shift clearly).

For example:
- **τίθημι** → Attic
- **θέτω** (3rd sg imperative, thematic form) → alternate usage

---

## 🔐 8. Special Notes on Compounds

μi-verbs form **many compounds**, and the prefixes can:
- affect accent (e.g., παρέδωκα)
- affect augment in past tenses (augment comes *after* the prefix)
  
Example:
> **παραδίδωμι** → "I hand over"  
> Aorist: **παρέδωκα** (prefix + augment + aorist stem)

---

## 🧰 9. Participles & Infinitives

### Present Active Participle (δίδους, διδοῦσα, διδόν)
> Nom. masc. sg: **δίδους**  
> Nom. fem. sg: **διδοῦσα**  
> Neut. nom/acc sg: **διδόν**

### Aorist Infinitive: δοῦναι  
Perfect Infinitive: δεδωκέναι
