## 📘 Attic Greek Verb Morphology Guide

### 🔹 1. Principal Parts (Τα Κύρια Ρήματα)

Every verb in Ancient Greek is defined by **six** principal parts:

| Part | Function                     | Example (λύω – "I release") |
|------|------------------------------|------------------------------|
| 1st  | Present Active/Middle        | λύω                         |
| 2nd  | Future Active/Middle         | λύσω                        |
| 3rd  | Aorist Active/Middle         | ἔλυσα                       |
| 4th  | Perfect Active               | λέλυκα                      |
| 5th  | Perfect Middle/Passive       | λέλυμαι                     |
| 6th  | Aorist/Future Passive        | ἐλύθην / λυθήσομαι          |

---

### 🔹 2. Tense Systems

Each tense has **aspectual** meaning, not always time-bound.

| Tense      | Aspect         | Usage                              |
|------------|----------------|-------------------------------------|
| Present    | Imperfective   | Repeated or ongoing actions         |
| Imperfect  | Imperfective   | Ongoing/repeated in past            |
| Future     | Imperfective   | Intended or future ongoing          |
| Aorist     | Perfective     | Simple, completed past action       |
| Perfect    | Stative        | Resulting state from past action    |
| Pluperfect | Stative        | Past of the perfect                 |
| Future Perfect | Stative    | Rare – future result from action    |

---

### 🔹 3. Voices

| Voice     | Meaning                        | Example                |
|-----------|--------------------------------|------------------------|
| Active    | Subject performs action         | λύω – I release        |
| Middle    | Subject acts on/for self        | λύομαι – I release for myself |
| Passive   | Subject is acted upon           | λύομαι (Pres), ἐλύθην (Aor) |

> 🔹 Middle is often **reflexive**, **reciprocal**, or denotes **interest** of the subject.

---

### 🔹 4. Moods

| Mood       | Usage                                      | Example      |
|------------|---------------------------------------------|--------------|
| Indicative | Statements and factual questions            | λύει          |
| Subjunctive| Purpose, fear, conditional, deliberation    | λύῃ           |
| Optative   | Wishes, potential, purpose, indirect speech | λύοι          |
| Imperative | Commands                                    | λῦε           |

---

### 🔹 5. Person & Number

| Person   | Singular | Plural |
|----------|----------|--------|
| 1st      | I        | We     |
| 2nd      | You      | You all|
| 3rd      | He/She/It| They   |

Forms are fully inflected for person and number.

---

### 🔹 6. Non-Finite Forms

#### 📌 Infinitives

| Tense     | Active   | Middle         | Passive        |
|-----------|----------|----------------|----------------|
| Present   | λύειν     | λύεσθαι         | λύεσθαι         |
| Aorist    | λῦσαι     | λύσασθαι        | λυθῆναι         |
| Perfect   | λελυκέναι | λελύσθαι        | λελύσθαι        |
| Future    | λύσειν    | λύσεσθαι        | λυθήσεσθαι      |

#### 📌 Participles

Fully declined like adjectives in gender, number, and case.

| Tense     | Active      | Middle         | Passive         |
|-----------|-------------|----------------|-----------------|
| Present   | λύων         | λυόμενος        | λυόμενος         |
| Aorist    | λύσας        | λυσάμενος       | λυθείς           |
| Perfect   | λελυκώς      | λελυμένος        | λελυμένος         |
| Future    | λύσων        | λυσόμενος       | λυθησόμενος       |

---

### 🔹 7. Augment, Reduplication, and Stems

- **Augment**: Added to **indicative** past tenses (imperfect, aorist, pluperfect).
  - Syllabic: ἐ + verb (ἐπαύον)
  - Temporal: vowel lengthening (ἀγείρω → ἤγειρα)

- **Reduplication**: For perfect tense.
  - λε-λυκα (doubling with ε)
  - If starts with vowel, use vowel + ι (ἵστημι → ἕστηκα)

---

### 🔹 8. Middle-Passive Nuances

| Use                  | Example               | Meaning                        |
|----------------------|-----------------------|--------------------------------|
| Reflexive            | λύομαι                 | I release myself               |
| Reciprocal (plural)  | φιλοῦνται               | They love each other           |
| Intransitive middle  | πορεύομαι              | I travel (not "am traveled")   |
| Passive              | ἐλύθη                  | Was released                   |

---

### 🔹 9. Contract Verbs (ε-contract, ο-contract, α-contract)

- Contracts occur in present system.
- Stem vowels merge with endings:

| Contract Type | Stem | 3rd Sing Pres | Example |
|---------------|------|---------------|---------|
| α-contract    | τιμᾱ– | τιμᾷ           | τιμάω  |
| ε-contract    | ποιε– | ποιεῖ           | ποιέω  |
| ο-contract    | δηλο– | δηλοῖ           | δηλόω  |

> Contract verbs follow normal rules in future/aorist.

---

### 🔹 10. μι-Verbs (Athematic Verbs)

#### Key Verbs:
- δίδωμι (give), τίθημι (put), ἵστημι (stand), ἵημι (send), δείκνυμι (show)

#### Features:
- Reduplication in present
- Use different endings:
  - δίδωμι, δίδως, δίδωσι, δίδομεν, δίδοτε, διδόασι
- Aorist uses **-κα** or **-ην** endings:
  - ἔδωκα, ἔθηκα, ἔστην, ἔβην

---

### 🔹 11. Verbal Adjectives

#### 📌 Future Passive Participle (-τέος)

- λύτεος = "to be released"
- Used to express **necessity or obligation**
