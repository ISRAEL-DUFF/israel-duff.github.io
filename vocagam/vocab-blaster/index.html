How to play

  • Choose a vocabulary set from the dropdown, then load it.
  • Click Start game to begin. A Greek word appears above the playfield.
  • Click the falling meaning that best matches the word to “shoot” it down.
  • Wrong shots or meanings that hit your ship cost you one life. You have three lives.
  • Survive as long as you can and aim for a high score!
