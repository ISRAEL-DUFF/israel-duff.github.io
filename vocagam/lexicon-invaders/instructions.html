How to Play

Objective

Your mission is to learn Greek vocabulary by shooting down the correct falling words that match the definitions listed on the left side of the screen.

Game Play

  • A list of English definitions will be displayed on the left. These are your target words.
  • Greek words will fall from the top of the screen.
  • You control a spaceship at the bottom. Your goal is to shoot the Greek words that correspond to your target definitions.

Scoring and Lives

  • Correct Hit: Shooting a target word scores you 10 points.
  • Incorrect Hit: Shooting a word that is NOT on your target list will cost you a life.
  • Missed Target: Letting a target word fall off the bottom of the screen will also cost you a life.
  • Distractors: Letting a non-target (distractor) word fall off the bottom has no penalty. You can safely ignore them.

Game Modes

  • Auto Mode: The game's speed will increase as you level up. This is a great way to challenge your recall speed.
  • Manual Mode: You can set the speed of the falling words using the slider at the top of the screen. This is perfect for a more relaxed learning session.

Controls

  • Left/Right Arrow Keys: Move your spaceship.
  • Spacebar: Fire your weapon.
