1. Executive Summary

Business Name: SkillNaija

Mission: To connect Nigerians with verified, skilled artisans (plumbers, electricians, tailors, etc.) instantly, reducing unemployment and service fraud.  


Vision: Become Nigeria’s largest on-demand service platform for trusted artisans by 2027.  

Target Market: Urban households, SMEs, and young professionals in Lagos, Abuja, and Port Harcourt.  

---

2. Market Analysis
Why It Works

- Demand: 60% of Nigerians struggle to find reliable artisans; middlemen inflate prices.  
- 
- Supply: 40M+ Nigerians work in the informal sector (tailors, mechanics, etc.).  
- ⁠
- Trends: Rising smartphone penetration (42%) and cashless payments.  

Competitors
- Tangerine.africa: Focuses on cleaning and beauty services.  

Target Users
- Customers: Middle-income households (₦150k–₦500k/month income), SMEs, students.  
- ⁠
- *Artisans*: Skilled workers seeking consistent income (e.g., Lagos tailors, Abuja electricians).  

---

3. Services Offered
Categories

1. Home Repairs: Plumbers, electricians, painters.  
2. Tailoring & Fashion: Tailors, shoemakers, hairstylists.  
3. Tech Services: Phone repairers, IT support.  
4. Cleaning: Home/office cleaners, laundry services.



App Features
- Geolocation: Find nearby artisans.  
- Instant Booking: Schedule same-day or future appointments.  
- Ratings & Reviews: 5-star system with detailed feedback.  
- Secure Payments: In-app wallet (Paystack/Flutterwave integration).  
- Verification: Artisan ID checks, skill tests, and background verification.  
- ⁠Reminder Alerts: To remind artisans and clients about their booking.

---

4. Business Model
Revenue Streams
1. *Commission per Booking*: 15% from customers (e.g., ₦1,500 on a ₦10,000 plumbing job).  
2. *Artisan Subscriptions*: ₦2,000/month for premium profiles (top search rankings).  
3. *Featured Listings**: ₦10,000/month for businesses (e.g., boutique tailors).  
4. **Corporate Partnerships**: ₦50,000/month for SMEs needing regular services (e.g., office cleaning).  

#### **Pricing Strategy**  
- **Customers**: No signup fee; pay only for booked services.  
- **Artisans**: Free basic profiles; subscriptions unlock premium features.  

---

#### **Partnerships**  
- **Verification Partners**: **Smile Identity** (KYC checks).  
- **Payment Security**: **Escrow payments** hold funds until job completion.  

---

### **8. Risk Analysis & Mitigation**  
- **Trust Issues**:  
  - **Mitigation**: Mandatory artisan verification (ID, skill test, background check).  
- **Payment Fraud**:  
  - **Mitigation**: Escrow system releases funds only after job approval.  
- **Low Adoption**:  
  - **Mitigation**: Offer ₦1,000 discounts for first-time users.  

---


Handling booking cancellations effectively:

1. Create a Clear Cancellation Policy
Policy Rules**:  
- **Free Cancellation Window**: Allow users to cancel for free up to **24 hours before the booking** without penalties.  
- **Late Cancellation Fees**:  
  - **Customer cancels within 24 hours**: Charge **20% of the service fee** (paid to the artisan as compensation).  
  - **Artisan cancels last-minute**: Penalize them with a **₦1,000 deduction** from future earnings and temporarily lower their visibility in search results.  
- **No-Show Policy**:  
  - **Customer no-show**: Full payment is released to the artisan.  
  - **Artisan no-show**: Full refund to the customer + free replacement artisan.  

**How to Communicate**:  
- Display the policy during booking.  
- Send SMS/WhatsApp reminders 48 and 24 hours before appointments.  

---

### **2. Automate Refunds & Penalties**  
**Technical Implementation**:  
- **Escrow System**: Use **Flutterwave Escrow** or **Paystack Subscriptions** to hold payments until job completion.  
- **Auto-Refunds**:  
  - If a customer cancels within the free window, refund 100% instantly.  
  - For late cancellations, deduct the penalty and release the rest to the customer.  
- **Artisan Compensation**: Automatically credit penalties (e.g., 20% fee) to the artisan’s wallet within 24 hours.  

---

### **3. Enable Rescheduling (Not Just Cancellation)**  
- **In-App Rescheduling**: Allow customers/artisans to reschedule bookings once for free (e.g., within 12 hours of cancellation).  
- **Penalty Avoidance**: No fees if rescheduled at least 6 hours before the original time.  

---

### **4. Build a Fair Rating System**  
- **Rate Cancellers**:  
  - Customers rate artisans if they cancel late.  
  - Artisans rate customers who frequently cancel.  
- **Consequences**:  
  - Users with >3 cancellations in a month get a warning; repeat offenders are temporarily suspended.  
  - Artisans with low ratings lose access to premium features.  

---

### **5. Offer Customer Support for Disputes**  
- **In-App Chat/Helpline**: Provide 24/7 support (via WhatsApp or phone) to resolve disputes.  
- **Escalation Process**:  
  1. User submits a complaint with evidence (e.g., screenshot of no-show).  
  2. Support team investigates within 24 hours.  
  3. Refund/compensation issued if the platform is at fault.  

---

### **6. Incentivize Reliability**  
- **For Artisans**:  
  - **Bonus Pay**: Offer a ₦500 bonus for zero cancellations in a month.  
  - **Priority Listings**: Reliable artisans appear higher in search results.  
- **For Customers**:  
  - **Loyalty Points**: Earn points for on-time bookings (redeemable for discounts).  
  - **Cancellation "Insurance"**: Offer a ₦300/month subscription for 1 free late cancellation.  

---

### **7. Use Technology to Reduce Cancellations**  
- **Automated Reminders**: Send SMS/WhatsApp reminders 48h, 24h, and 1h before appointments.  
- **Calendar Sync**: Let users sync bookings with Google/Apple Calendar.  
- **Real-Time GPS Tracking**: Allow customers to track the artisan’s arrival in real time.  

---

### **Example Workflow**:  
1. **Customer books a plumber** via SkillNaija for ₦15,000 (held in escrow).  
2. **Customer cancels 6 hours before**:  
   - Free cancellation → full refund.  
3. **Customer cancels 2 hours before**:  
   - 20% fee (₦3,000) paid to the plumber; ₦12,000 refunded.  
4. **Plumber cancels last-minute**:  
   - Customer gets full refund + ₦1,000 discount on next booking.  
   - Plumber pays a ₦1,000 penalty.  

---

### **Cultural Nuances to Address**  
- **Flexible Payments**: Allow customers to pay a **50% deposit** upfront (reduces cancellations).  
- **Localized Support**: Hire customer service reps fluent in Yoruba, Hausa, and Igbo.  
- **Trust Building**: Partner with local influencers to vouch for your platform’s fairness.  

---

### **Tools to Implement This**  
- **Escrow Payments**: Flutterwave, Paystack.  
- **SMS Automation**: Termii, Africa’s Talking.  
- **Dispute Mediation**: Zendesk or Freshdesk for ticketing.

# PRODUCT SPEC

**Product Specification Document**

**App Name (TBD)**: On-demand Artisan Services Platform for Nigeria

---

### 1. **Overview**

A mobile-first platform to connect Nigerians with verified, skilled artisans (plumbers, electricians, tailors, etc.) for instant or scheduled service bookings. Inspired by Bolt/Uber, the app will offer real-time artisan discovery, secure payments, trust mechanisms, and user support.

---

### 2. **Target Users**

* **Customers**: Individuals seeking quick and reliable artisan services.
* **Artisans**: Skilled service providers looking for verified, paid jobs.
* **Admins**: Internal team for oversight, support, and dispute resolution.

---

### 3. **Core Features (MVP)**

#### A. **Customer App**

* Sign up / Login (phone, email, Google/Facebook)
* Location access (to show nearby artisans)
* Search & filter artisans by skill, rating, price
* View artisan profile & availability
* Book service (same-day or scheduled)
* In-app wallet & secure payment (Paystack/Flutterwave)
* Escrow payment logic
* Booking summary & reminders (SMS/WhatsApp)
* Rate and review artisan
* View booking history

#### B. **Artisan App**

* Sign up with ID, skill, and background check upload
* Profile & certification management
* Accept/decline bookings
* View booking calendar
* Escrow payout to wallet after service
* Earnings dashboard
* Ratings and customer feedback
* Performance tips & bonus notifications

#### C. **Admin Dashboard**

* View/manage customers & artisans
* Artisan approval (ID, skill test, background verification)
* Handle disputes
* Manage cancellations and penalties
* Monitor transactions & ratings

---

### 4. **Advanced Features (Post-MVP)**

* Real-time GPS tracking of artisan en route
* Subscription model for cancellation insurance
* Loyalty & reward point system
* Automated penalties for no-shows
* Cancellation policy engine
* In-app chat (via WhatsApp API)
* Calendar sync
* Bonus for reliability, fraud detection alerts

---

### 5. **Cancellation & Refund Logic**

**Customer Cancellations**:

* > 24h before booking: Free
* <24h: 20% fee paid to artisan
* No-show: Full payment released to artisan

**Artisan Cancellations**:

* <24h: ₦1,000 deducted, lower visibility
* No-show: Full refund + replacement

**Rescheduling**:

* Allowed once, no penalty if >6h notice

**Automated Refunds**:

* Use Flutterwave Escrow API
* Time-based logic to refund or transfer payments automatically
